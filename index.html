FROM ORDER FLOW TO INSTINCT

He doesn't read the news.
He feels the order book.

The Degeneret Fly sits at the intersection of neuroscience and market microstructure. A live order book becomes sensory input. Neural activity builds, crosses a threshold, and moves through the circuit. The visible branches let you follow that moment.

Explore the anatomical source ↗
  1. 01 / SENSE

    Buying pressure, a full minute of price movement, and changing spreads feed the fly's sensory channels. His monitor follows the same live market.

  2. 02 / THINK

    A leaky integrate-and-fire circuit turns those inputs into spikes. Lightning travels over reconstructed MaleCNS neuron branches; green marks descending motor activity.

  3. 03 / LEARN

    After sixty seconds, each prediction meets the next observed price. Small weight updates adjust the next decision. An edge must cover the spread before the fly adds a fill to his ledger.

Inside the experiment: anatomy, chemistry & learning

The brain uses 90 source-derived anatomical compartments and 42 sampled MaleCNS v1.0 neuron skeletons, including DNge104 left and right. This is a subset of the neuron reconstructions. The functional circuit and its animation overlay are authored models; the dataset records anatomy, not live brain recordings.

Dopamine, octopamine, serotonin and acetylcholine are dimensionless activity indices driven by prediction error, volatility, restraint and sensory drive. They are not chemical concentrations. Octopamine is used for the insect stress-response channel. Learning is an online prediction experiment, with no claim of improved returns. The ledger applies the fly's decisions to observed prices; Polymarket's tape displays exchange-reported trades.
